Sources and credits

Where the content in Istiqamah comes from, and the licences it is shared under. Thank you to everyone whose work makes the app possible.

1Sources

Prayer times

All Ceylon Jamiyyathul Ulama (ACJU), from its published timetable. Times the timetable lacks are calculated and marked so in the app. www.acju.lk/prayer-times

Quran text

Tanzil Quran Text (Uthmani, Version 1.1). Copyright (C) 2007-2026 Tanzil Project. Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Shown unchanged; updates at tanzil.net. Full notice.

Transliteration

en.transliteration, Tanzil Project (tanzil.net), via alquran.cloud.

English meaning

Talal Itani, “Quran in English: Clear, Pure, Easy to Read”. Free to use and share, as the translator states. Shown unchanged. www.clearquran.com

Recitation

Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary (also his teaching recitation), Mohamed Siddiq Al-Minshawi and Abdul Basit Abdus Samad, streamed from EveryAyah. Nothing is saved on the phone. everyayah.com

Adhan sounds

“The Adhan - Muslim Call to Prayer - Aaqib Azeez” by Atcovi, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0; silence at the start trimmed (source). “Azan” by Andrewler, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (source).

Related verses

Quranpedia.net’s topic index (topics.json, version 2026-11-14): ayat that share a topic. quranpedia.net

Duas and hadith

Hisn al-Muslim (hisnmuslim.com), fawazahmed0/hadith-api and alquran.cloud. Every item shows its reference in the app.

Quotes after each prayer

Ayat from Talal Itani’s Clear Quran; hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, via fawazahmed0/hadith-api.

Map data

Town locations and the mosque list: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. More mosques from Overture Maps Foundation, CDLA-Permissive-2.0. The mosque list is published at /data/mosques.json. Full notice.

Fonts

Amiri, Fraunces, Nunito and Noto Sans Tamil, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
