2Information stored on your phone

To work, the app saves these on your phone only:

  • the prayers you tick off, make-up (qada) prayers and rest days
  • your favourite duas
  • your settings, such as your zone or district, language, theme and alert choices

The developer cannot see this information. It stays until you delete it: clear the app's storage in Settings → Apps → Istiqamah → Storage, or uninstall the app.

Backups you make. If you use the app's backup feature, it creates a file with the information above and lets you choose where to save or send it. That file goes only where you choose. If Android's own backup to your Google account is turned on, Android may include the app's settings and log in that backup, under Google's privacy policy.

3Permissions and why they're used

Approximate location

To find your nearest ACJU prayer-time zone. Asked only when you choose it; you can pick your district by hand instead. The location is used on the phone and isn't stored or sent.

Notifications

For prayer alerts and the panel that shows all five prayer times.

Alarms and reminders

So prayer alerts arrive at the exact minute.

Full-screen alarm

So the Fajr wake-up alarm can show over the lock screen while it rings.

Run at start-up and keep the phone awake briefly

To set alerts again after the phone restarts, and to finish setting them.

Internet

Only for streaming Quran recitation. Prayer times, the Quran text, duas and alerts all work offline.

Media playback in the background

So recitation keeps playing when the screen is locked or you leave the app.

You can refuse any of these. The app still works; only the related feature is limited.

4Other services

everyayah.com. When you play a recitation, your phone asks everyayah.com for that audio over a secure (HTTPS) connection. Like any website, it receives your IP address and which recitation you asked for. The audio is streamed and not saved on your phone. The app sends nothing else to it.

Google Play. Google provides the download and updates, and collects crash reports for all apps, under Google's privacy policy. The developer may see anonymous, combined statistics from Google Play, such as install counts and crash reports, which do not identify you.

Links you open. Some buttons, such as this policy, the Play Store page, credits and the feedback email, open in your browser or email app. The app sends nothing with them; those apps and websites follow their own privacy policies.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.

5Security

Your information stays on your phone, protected by Android's own app storage. Nothing is kept on any server by the developer. Recitation streams over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

6Your rights

The developer collects no personal data, so there is nothing held about you to access, correct or delete. You control everything the app stores and can delete it at any time, as described above. If you have a question or concern under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, or any other privacy law, contact the developer below.

7Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

8Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, for example by adding a feature that uses the internet, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
